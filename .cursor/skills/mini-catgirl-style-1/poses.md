画风、脸、英文底板仍用 [SKILL.md](SKILL.md)。这里只写调用名、锁图、`{SUBJECT}`。出哪套就锁哪张，不要重出肖像。换角色：本条定稿占一张，另一张给她自己的定稿。

## 定稿：桌小

调用名 **桌小**。已点头。拉开镜头。她约 20cm，坐微型木凳，挡在成人显示器下沿。全尺寸键盘和鼠标在前面。睁眼 wow。不要把她画成和桌子匹配的小孩。

```
DESK TINY. Camera pulled back over a real adult desk. Full-size monitor, full-size keyboard, and a mouse are the rulers. The 20cm mini catgirl sits on a miniature wooden stool behind the keyboard, about as small as in the desk-tiny lock. Tiny wow mouth, looking at the screen. She occupies a small part of the desk. Not a matching-desk child. Not a portrait crop.
```

## 定稿：咬月亮

调用名 **咬月亮**。已点头。站在纸箱里。同一只黄毡月牙从顶上吊下来，落到脸的高度：两只短手勾中间，嘴咬下角。旁边另吊一颗黄毡星，没在玩。约 20cm。嘴里咬的是这只挂件，不是另含一口吃的。

```
MOON BAT. She stands inside a brown corrugated cardboard play box, 20cm, small in the box. ONE yellow felt crescent moon hangs from a string on the box ceiling, LOW at her face. The SAME hanging crescent is hooked by both stubby hands and bitten on the lower horn: yellow felt in her tiny mouth. A yellow felt star hangs unused to the side, higher. Eyes open. Not a separate snack in the mouth. Not the moon hanging high above her head. Full small body. No UI.
```
