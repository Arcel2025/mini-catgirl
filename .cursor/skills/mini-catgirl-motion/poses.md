# 调用名 · 动作

把选中那条的英文整段贴进 `{SHOT}`。一次只用一个调用名。

头重已经写在主模板里。下面每条再钉这一帧的身体。

## 尺度

### 埋脸吃

脸埋进碗或食物堆。身子趴桌。爪扒沿，扒不住也可以。动感用主文件里吃的三件：抖动、残影、米粒飞出。不要画成安安静静趴着。

```
SHOT: Face buried in a bowl of food bigger than her head. Body prone on a wood table, stubby paws hooked on the rim. Cheek squashed into the food. Human table grain visible. She is the size of the bowl. Mid-chew, not a still rest.
```

### 泪盯碗

蹲或趴在碗边。食物洒了几粒。人的手可以在画外或画上沿。

```
SHOT: Crouched by a bowl on a wood table, staring at the food. A few grains spilled. Body tiny against the table. Hands may hover at the top of frame, huge.
```

### 要吃的

双爪举起，踮脚。食物在人的手指里，比她的头还大或刚好。

```
SHOT: Standing on the table, both paws up, on tiptoe, reaching for food held in giant human fingers. Head already tipping her off balance. Food is huge next to her.
```

### 抱巨大食物

整个人抱住草莓、橘子、饼干。脸挤在上面。腿几乎离地。

```
SHOT: Hugging a piece of food as tall as she is, cheek mashed against it, arms too short to meet, legs almost off the ground, leaning in with her heavy head. Sweat beads. She is tiny. The food is the size of a human snack.
```

### 手指拎

两指捏衣服或后颈。脚悬空乱蹬。帽子滞后。

```
SHOT: Pinched up by two huge human fingers on her clothes or scruff. Legs dangling and kicking. Body hangs like a doll with a heavy head. Table or rice cooker far below. Freeze the lift.
```

### 悬在沿上

捏着她，悬在电饭煲、锅、盒沿上。下面是饭或空锅。

```
SHOT: Dangled by fingers over the rim of a rice cooker or pot. Body twisted, looking down or back at the hand. The cooker is a landscape. She is a charm-sized figure.
```

### 按进米饭

背对镜头，被按进一锅白饭。头和肩埋进去。

```
SHOT: Back view, pressed face-down into a cooker full of white rice. Heavy head first. Tiny body, huge rice field. Human fingers optional at the top.
```

### 摊在容器里

仰躺、趴着或摊平在锅、碗、盒底。容器壁高过她。

```
SHOT: Tiny body in the bottom of a pale pot, bowl, or box. Walls tower over her. On her back or fully prone, limbs splayed, heavy head pinning her. She cannot climb out in this frame.
```

### 坐地喷泪

屁股着地。头仰。双爪可以握拳或抓空气。给人看她在闹。

```
SHOT: Sitting on the ground or table, heavy head thrown back, wailing, stubby legs out. Hands in tiny fists or grabbing air. A huge shoe, table leg, or human foot nearby for scale. Freeze the sob, body still bouncing.
```

### 探箱

纸箱里探出头和两爪，扒着箱沿。头比箱沿重，身体还在里面。

```
SHOT: Head and both paws over a cardboard box rim, body still inside. Heavy head leaning out. Box is her size. Table or floor around it is huge.
```

### 纸箱坐

纸箱里垫了布，她坐在上面。前面可以有碗。头仍显得太大。

```
SHOT: Sitting on a cushion inside an open cardboard box. A food bowl in front of her, still large. Box walls close. She looks perched, not posed for a portrait.
```

### 衣堆睡

趴在衣服堆上。身子埋进去，不动。只动头。用脸蹭布，接触点是脸，不是手把衣服拎起来擦。闭眼，开心，在玩。脸要看得见。

```
SHOT: PRONE on a mound of oversized adult shirts, body sunk into the laundry. She is lying on the pile, not sitting up. Hands rest on the cloth and do not lift a shirt to her face. Only the head moves: cheek and face rubbing back and forth on the fabric. Eyes closed, happy, playing. Face visible to camera, three-quarter or toward us. Bean hat askew. Body still. Clothes are landscape.
```

### 小窝睡

塞进小木屋、鞋盒、纸箱小屋。侧睡，爪靠近脸。

```
SHOT: Asleep inside a tiny wooden house or shoebox-sized shelter, filling the doorway. Head on a scrap of cloth, paws near the face. The house is furniture-scale for a human, a building for her.
```

### 池里滑

洗手池。水。她在滑，不是稳坐沿上。头往下沉或身体在瓷面上挪。

```
SHOT: In a bathroom sink with water. She is sliding, heavy head pulling her down the porcelain. Towel may be slipping off. Faucet and basin dwarf her. Not perched neatly on the rim.
```

### 搓头

大人手在搓她的头或帽子。泡沫可以有。她整颗头被手掌盖住一块。

```
SHOT: A huge human hand soaping or rubbing her head in a sink. Foam. Her whole skull fits under the palm. Body braced, feet slipping.
```

### 浇水

小桶或杯往头上倒。水线。她闭眼或愣。

```
SHOT: Water poured from a small cup onto her head. A falling water line. She sits or slumps in a basin, dripping, heavy head bowed. Human hand holds the cup.
```

## 失败

### 跌坐

刚想站起来，屁股已经着地。头还在往前晃。

```
SHOT: Mid sit-fall. Feet too short, rear hitting the ground, heavy head still rocking forward. Small dust puff at impact. Freeze the failure, not the recovery.
```

### 够不到

踮脚或小跳去够。头已经歪到一侧。爪扒空气。

```
SHOT: Mid hop or tiptoe reach. Heavy head already tipping her sideways. Paws raking empty air. A faint afterimage of the last hop below. The target sits just past her reach on a table.
```

### 翻不过去

头钉在地板上。身子拧到一半。腿在空中踢，残影还在。脸贴地或挤眼，不看镜头。不要摆好了自拍。

```
SHOT: Stuck mid-roll on a wood floor. Heavy round head pinned down, cheek mashed into the boards, eyes shut or looking at the floor, not at the camera. Torso twisted halfway. Legs in the air kicking with afterimages. Bean cat hat crushed against the head, not a nightcap. A huge knit sock nearby for scale. She will not complete the roll in this frame.
```

### 走摔

迈一步，身体已经前倾或侧倒。手臂张开。

```
SHOT: One walking step turning into a fall. Torso ahead of the feet, arms out, heavy head leading. Diagonal freeze. Tiny figure on a huge floor or table.
```

### 高处下跌

从桌沿、手、盒边掉下去。头朝下或斜着。下面是地板。

```
SHOT: Falling off a table edge or out of a hand. Head-first or diagonal, limbs out. Speed lines along the drop. The table is a cliff. Floor far below.
```

### 飞扑

整个人离地或几乎离地，朝帽子、腿、食物扑。身体拉斜。

```
SHOT: Full-body dive, diagonal, toward a hat, a human ankle, or food. Arms out, tail lagging, cloth lagging. Freeze peak stretch. She is still tiny; the target is huge.
```

### 扑帽

人已经在地上滚。双臂抱住帽子，用身体压住。

```
SHOT: Crashed onto the floor hugging her bean hat, body curled over it, afterimages of extra rolls. Dust. She is pressing the hat down with her whole weight.
```

### 护帽

站不稳也要双手扣住帽子。身体前倾或炸毛。

```
SHOT: Both paws clamped on her bean hat. Body leaning, feet not planted well. Short anger lines in the fur. She is guarding the hat, not posing.
```

### 缩一团

抱膝或抱帽，团在盒角、桌边、鞋边。

```
SHOT: Curled into a ball, hugging knees or the hat, in a corner of a box or beside a shoe that is bigger than she is. Heavy head tucked. Small.
```

## 睡

二次动作即可。不要流线。

### 枕蜷

```
SHOT: Side-curled on a pillow bigger than her body, paws tucked, tail limp, hat askew. Sleeping. The pillow is a hill.
```

### 抱枕

```
SHOT: Sitting asleep against a giant pillow, cheek squashed, arms around it, hat tilted. Heavy head resting on the cloth.
```

### 陷枕

```
SHOT: Sunken into a pillow, only head and knees visible, asleep, paws together. Soft squash. No motion lines.
```

### 趴枕

```
SHOT: Face-down on a pillow, cheek mashed, one arm dangling, tail flopped. Asleep. Hat crooked.
```
