# 调用名 · 脸

把选中那条贴进 `{FACE}`。一次一张脸。耳和帽子跟脸走。

源里的伤心六脸和恐惧五步，已经改成迷你猫娘能用的。

## 哭

### 难过

眉下垂。眼圆，水光。嘴扁或轻轻下撇。一两滴泪停在脸上。

```
FACE: Sad. Downturned inner brows, round wet eyes, flat wobbling mouth, one or two tears on the cheeks. Ears slightly down. Hat may sit crooked.
```

### 嚎啕

嘴张成方或大圆。眼挤成线或大睁。泪往外喷成弧。鼻涕或唾液可以有一点。

```
FACE: Wailing. Mouth a big square or circle, eyes squeezed or wide, tears shooting out in arcs, spit flecks. Ears flat. Shoulders implied pumping. Comic cry, still a still image.
```

### 不忿哭

眉拧。泪在。嘴是生气的形，不是软的扁嘴。像在骂。

```
FACE: Angry crying. Knitted brows, glare through tears, mouth clenched or shouting small. Ears half up, bristled. She is mad while crying.
```

### 委屈哭

眼大，高光多。泪在下睑。嘴委屈地下弯。看镜头或看人的手。

```
FACE: Wronged-puppy cry. Oversized wet eyes, tears on the lower lid, trembling downturned mouth, looking up at a huge person. Ears down.
```

### 忧伤

泪少。眼看下方。嘴安静。没有喷泪。

```
FACE: Quiet sorrow. Eyes looking down, few tears, still mouth, low energy. Ears dropped. No spray.
```

### 吓哭

眼大。泪刚出来。嘴张着吸气。像被吓出声之后马上要哭。

```
FACE: Startle-cry. Wide eyes, first tears leaving the eyes, mouth open on a gasp, ears snapping back. Freeze the shock turning into a sob.
```

## 怕

源：拧眉、眼珠缩小、瞳孔缩小、嘴发抖、吓汗、脸色发冷。五步可以叠在一张脸上。

### 怕

```
FACE: Fear. Brows pinched, irises small, pupils pinprick, mouth a shaking oval or grimace, cold sweat flying off the temple, ears glued to the head. Cheeks a little purple-grey. She is 20cm and the world is huge.
```

### 愣

被拎起来、帽飞了、食物被拿走的第一帧。

```
FACE: Stun. Eyes round and empty for one beat, mouth a small open O, brows up. Ears halfway. No smile.
```

### 空眼摊

累垮、摊在容器里、哭完没力气。

```
FACE: Spent. Half-lidded empty eyes, mouth slack, no extra tears, ears limp. The face of someone who already lost.
```

## 怒和护

### 怒吼

眼挤。嘴大张。短炸毛。给护帽、跺脚、锤空气。

```
FACE: Yell. Eyes squeezed shut or glaring slits, mouth wide, short bristling lines off the hair and ears. Tiny fists implied. Not a cool battle face.
```

### 使劲咬

抱巨大食物时。眼挤，牙或嘴贴上去，汗。

```
FACE: Straining bite. Eyes shut, mouth latched onto huge food, sweat, mashed cheek. Effort, not cute tasting.
```

## 吃和要

### 满足闭眼

埋脸吃。眼闭。眉放松。可以有一点泪是辣或太满足，不要哭相。

```
FACE: Bliss eat. Eyes closed, relaxed brows, mouth hidden in food, cheeks full. Peaceful greed.
```

### 星星眼

看见吃的、看见橘子、看见饼干。瞳孔大，高光。

```
FACE: Food sparkle. Huge pupils, bright highlights, mouth slightly open, ears up. Looking at food, not at the camera for a pinup.
```

## 睡和忍

### 蹭脸开心

衣堆里用脸蹭布。眼闭。嘴是开心的形。眉放松。没有泪。不是哭。

```
FACE: Happy nuzzle. Eyes closed in pleasure, relaxed brows, a small content smile, cheek squashed into cloth. No tears. No crying. Playing, not sad, not asleep.
```

### 闭眼睡

```
FACE: Asleep. Closed eyes, slack mouth, maybe a drip of drool. Ears relaxed. Hat askew if she has one.
```

### 闭眼忍

浇水、搓头、被拎但不看镜头。

```
FACE: Enduring. Eyes shut tight, mouth a small line or grimace, dripping water or foam. Not enjoying a spa. Ears pinned.
```
